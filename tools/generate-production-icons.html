📋 Installation Instructions

  1. Click "Download All Icons" button above
  2. Three PNG files will download to your browser's download folder
  3. Move the downloaded files to the icons/ folder in your project:
    • icon16.png → icons/icon16.png
    • icon48.png → icons/icon48.png
    • icon128.png → icons/icon128.png
  4. Reload your extension in chrome://extensions/
  5. The new icons should appear immediately!
